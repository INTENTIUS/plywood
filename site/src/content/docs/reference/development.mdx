## The chant pin

`@intentius/chant` and every chant lexicon are pinned to one exact version in `package.json` and `packages/plywood/package.json`, and `just bundle-check` holds the two together. [Versions and pins](/plywood/reference/versions-and-pins/) names the pinned release. A chant feature newer than the pin goes behind a feature-detect with a fallback; `packages/plywood/src/chant-next.ts` lists those from chant#4624 to chant#4636.

To build plywood against chant from source:

```sh
just chant-local              # packs chant's origin/main from a worktree next to the chant checkout
just chant-local ../chant     # packs that chant directory
just chant-local --reset      # back to the pin
```

Run `--reset` before committing a lock file.

## Draft pages

A guide whose feature needs a newer chant or plunge than plywood pins carries `draft: true` and the issues under `needs:` in its front matter. `just site-serve` shows drafts, and a production build leaves them out. In `site/`, `node scripts/check-needs.mjs --list` prints each need with its pages. With `--closed` it names the needs whose issues have closed. A page with no needs left drops `draft: true` and publishes.
